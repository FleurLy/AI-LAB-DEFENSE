## Risk explanation

Key indicators include complete SPF, DKIM, and DMARC authentication failure; a mismatch between the From domain \(8617.com\) and Reply\-To domain \(secure\-auth\-login.top\); and sender domains that do not match the claimed organization. The message pressures the recipient with a “final reminder” and threatened account suspension, while invoking account\-review and anti\-fraud authority. Generic placeholders and inconsistent dates further undermine credibility. No destination URL was extracted, so the link itself could not be analyzed. No attachments, QR codes, payment request, or secrecy request were identified, but that does not reduce the substantial credential\-phishing risk.

## Recommended actions

- Do not click the login link, reply to the message, or provide credentials or personal information.
- Verify any account issue independently by opening the organization’s known official website or app—not through information or links in the message—and contacting support through a trusted channel if needed.
- Report the message as phishing to the organization’s security or IT team and through the mail provider’s reporting function, then quarantine or delete it according to policy.
- If the link was already opened, stop interacting with the page and notify the security or IT team promptly.
- If credentials or personal information were submitted, immediately change the affected password through the official site, change any reused passwords, enable multi\-factor authentication where available, and follow the organization’s incident\-response guidance.
